A Boy Named Drew is a solo acoustic tribute celebrating the music and legacy of Johnny Cash.

Performed by Drew Wilson, the show features many of Cash's most beloved songs, including classics from Sun Records, Columbia Records, and the American Recordings era. Audiences can expect an authentic, heartfelt performance built around acoustic guitar, storytelling, and the timeless songs that made Johnny Cash one of America's most enduring artists.

Meet Drew Wilson

Drew Wilson is a singer and guitarist who brings a love of Americana, folk, country, and rock music to the stage.

A Boy Named Drew pays tribute to one of America's most recognizable musical voices with a performance centered around the songs themselves.

The goal isn't to simply recreate a Johnny Cash record.

It's to bring the songs into the room and let the stories speak for themselves.

Perfect for theaters, festivals, churches, wineries, community events, senior living communities, private parties, and country music venues, The Man in Black Acoustic Show brings the spirit of Johnny Cash to audiences of all ages.
